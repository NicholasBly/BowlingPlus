How the wrap sheet works

A 2:1 picture (2048×1024 is ideal) is the pin's surface unrolled like a sheet of paper: left to right goes once around the pin and the left and right edges meet; top is the top of the head, bottom is the base. Side A is at ¼ of the width, side B at ¾. The neck is roughly 8–11 in on the guide. The pin's flat underside keeps the game's own look.
